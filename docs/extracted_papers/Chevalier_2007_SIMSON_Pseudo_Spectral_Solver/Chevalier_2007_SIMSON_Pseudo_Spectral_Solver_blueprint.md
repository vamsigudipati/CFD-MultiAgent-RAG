## 1. Physical Problem Statement
This publication is a technical report and user manual for **SIMSON**, a high-order Fortran-based pseudo-spectral solver for the 3D incompressible Navier–Stokes equations in wall-bounded flow geometries (Blasius/Falkner–Skan/Falkner–Skan–Cooke boundary layer flows, plane Poiseuille channel flows, and plane Couette flows). The solver uses a velocity–vorticity formulation ($v$--$\omega$), discretized via Fourier expansions in horizontal wall-parallel directions ($x, z$) and Chebyshev polynomial expansions in the wall-normal direction ($y$), with semi-implicit Crank–Nicolson / 3rd–4th order Runge–Kutta time integration. It implements standard physical turbulence models for Large-Eddy Simulations (Dynamic Smagorinsky, High-Pass Filtered Smagorinsky, Relaxation-Term ADM-RT), Selective Frequency Damping (SFD), Magnetohydrodynamics (MHD) under low magnetic Reynolds numbers, and fringe-region forcing for spatial boundary layer development.
