This paper (*"Discovering Symbolic Models from Deep Learning with Inductive Biases"* by Cranmer et al.) introduces a general methodology for distilling explicit, closed-form symbolic equations from deep learning models using inductive biases. The framework trains Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) and Hamiltonian Graph Networks with sparse or bottlenecked latent representations, then extracts symbolic relations via symbolic regression ($e.g.$, Eureqa, PySR). The approach is evaluated on $N$-body particle dynamics (predicting particle accelerations and scalar interaction potentials under spring, gravitational, electrostatic, and discontinuous forces) and cosmological dark matter simulations (predicting halo overdensities $\delta$), rather than fluid dynamics or continuum fluid mechanics problems.
